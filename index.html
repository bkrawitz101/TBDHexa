The Magic of the Oracle

1. The I Ching (The Map of Life)

Imagine the universe is a giant kitchen, and it only uses 8 magical ingredients: Heaven, Earth, Fire, Water, Wind, Thunder, Mountain, and Lake.

When you mix two of these ingredients together—like mixing the bright heat of Fire with the cool breeze of Wind—they create a special recipe. In the I Ching, we call this recipe a Hexagram.

There are 64 possible recipes in total. By choosing two ingredients (your Inner and Outer worlds), the Oracle finds the exact recipe that tells the story of what is happening in your life right now!

2. The Gene Keys (Your Inner Superpowers)

If the I Ching is the map of the universe, the Gene Keys are the map inside of you. They teach us that everyone has superpowers, but sometimes those powers are hiding.

Each of the 64 Hexagrams is also a Gene Key, which is split into three levels of awareness:

🌑 The Shadow: This is how you feel when you are scared, stuck, or hiding. (Like a seed buried in the dark soil).

🌱 The Gift: This is your superpower waking up! It happens when you open your heart and face your fears.

✨ The Siddhi: This is pure magic. It is your absolute highest, brightest self blooming like a beautiful flower.
